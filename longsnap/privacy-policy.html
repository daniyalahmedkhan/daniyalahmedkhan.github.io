Privacy Policy

Long Snap for Android · Effective date: September 27, 2026

In short
  • Your screenshots and screen content stay on your device. We never upload, collect or see them.
  • The Accessibility service is used only to perform scroll gestures. It does not read anything on your screen.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase to improve the app.
  • We do not show ads, do not use the Advertising ID and do not sell your data.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Long Snap (the "App"), published by App Cluster ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the App. Long Snap lets you create long (scrolling) screenshots of other apps, either automatically (Auto scroll & stitch) or by tapping to capture each screen yourself (Manual snap & stitch). The captured screens are joined into one image on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit:

  • Screenshots, captured frames or the final stitched images.
  • Any text, images or other content shown on your screen, in any app.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or files.
  • The Advertising ID. The App does not request it and does not show ads.

All screen capture and image stitching happens entirely on your device. Final images are saved only to your own gallery (in the Pictures/LongSnap folder) and are shared with other apps only when you choose to use the Share option.

3. Information we collect

To understand how the App is used and to fix crashes, the App uses two Google services. The information they collect is anonymous and does not include any screen content.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Collects anonymous usage statistics, such as:

  • App interactions (for example, which screens are opened and whether a capture or tutorial was started or completed).
  • A randomly generated app instance ID (not linked to your identity).
  • General device and app information, such as device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate country.

Firebase Crashlytics

If the App crashes or runs into an error, Crashlytics collects:

  • Crash logs and diagnostic information (stack traces and the state of the App at the time of the crash).
  • Device and app information, such as device model, Android version and app version.
  • A Firebase installation ID used to group crash reports.

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We use it only to measure how the App is used, find and fix bugs, and improve performance. We do not use it to identify you and we do not sell or share it with anyone else.

Google processes this data under its own terms. You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

4. Permissions the App uses

PermissionWhy it is needed
Screen capture (MediaProjection) Takes screenshots of the screen you are viewing, only after you accept Android's screen-capture dialog, and only while a capture is running. The captured images stay on your device.
Accessibility service Used only in Auto scroll & stitch mode to perform swipe gestures that scroll the app you are capturing. It does not read window content, does not listen to accessibility events, and does not collect or transmit any on-screen text or personal data. You are shown a clear explanation before being asked to enable it, and you can turn it off at any time in Android Settings.
Display over other apps Shows the floating capture button so you can start and stop a capture while inside another app.
Foreground service & notifications Keeps the capture and the floating button running while you use other apps, and shows a notification so you always know the App is active.
Storage (Android 9 and below only) Saves the final image to your gallery. On newer Android versions no storage permission is needed.

5. Data retention

Screenshots are kept only on your device, for as long as you keep them. Temporary frames created during a capture are deleted automatically after stitching. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period according to Firebase's data retention settings, after which they are deleted automatically.

6. Your choices

  • You can disable the Accessibility service, overlay permission or notifications at any time in Android Settings.
  • You can delete any image saved by the App from your gallery at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App stops all data collection.
  • You can ask us to delete analytics or crash data related to your installation by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Screen content never leaves your device. Analytics and crash data are transmitted securely over HTTPS and stored by Google Firebase. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we collect.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
