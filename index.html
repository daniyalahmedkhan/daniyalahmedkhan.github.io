Long Snap: Auto Screenshots

Scrolling screenshots in one tap. Auto-stitched, no ads, stays on your phone.

  • No ads, and screenshots never leave your device

Ticketly: Event Ticket Scanner

Professional QR ticket scanning and attendee check-in for event organizers.

  • Scan QR tickets at the door with your phone
  • Check attendees in and manage the guest list
  • Works with WordPress sites using Event Tickets Plus and The Events Calendar

Ticketly is an independent app and is not affiliated with Event Tickets Plus or The Events Calendar.
