What is Long Snap?

Long Snap is a free long screenshot app for Android. It turns a whole scrolling page into one tall image, so you can capture a full conversation, a long article or a complete receipt in a single screenshot.

A normal screenshot only shows what fits on the screen. Long Snap scrolls the page, captures each screen and stitches them together in order. The app has no ads, adds no watermark and needs no account.

How it works

How to take a long screenshot on Android

Long Snap gives you two ways to capture a page. Both end with one clean, stitched image.

Hands-free

Auto scroll & stitch

Long Snap scrolls and captures for you.

  1. Tap Auto scroll & stitch to show the floating capture button.
  2. Open any app or web page you want to capture.
  3. Tap the button. Long Snap scrolls the page and captures each screen.
  4. It stops by itself at the end of the page, or tap the button to stop early.
  5. Your long screenshot is stitched and ready to save or share.

Best for long pages that scroll normally, such as chats, articles and feeds.

Full control

Manual snap & stitch

You scroll, and tap for each screen you want.

  1. Tap Manual snap & stitch.
  2. Scroll the page yourself and tap the button for each screen you want.
  3. Tap Done. Long Snap joins your screens into one clean image.

Best when you only want parts of a page, or in apps where automatic scrolling does not work well. Manual mode does not need the accessibility service.

Privacy

Your screenshots never leave your phone

A screenshot tool sees whatever is on your screen, so it should be clear about what it does with it. Long Snap keeps everything on your device.

Captured on your device

All capturing and stitching happens on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Saved only to your gallery

Finished images go to Pictures/LongSnap. They are shared only when you choose to share them.

Screen content is never read

Long Snap does not read, collect or upload anything shown on your screen.

No advertising

There are no ads in the app and no advertising ID is used.

The app sends anonymous crash reports and usage statistics so bugs can be fixed. These contain no screen content. Read the full Long Snap privacy policy.

Permissions

Why Long Snap asks for each permission

Long Snap asks only for what a scrolling screenshot needs, and each permission has one job.

Screen capture

Used only while you are taking a screenshot, and only after you accept Android's own screen-capture prompt.

Needed in Auto and Manual mode.

Display over other apps

Shows the floating capture button, so you can start and stop a capture while you are inside another app.

Needed in Auto and Manual mode.

Accessibility service

Performs the swipe gestures that scroll the page for you. It does not read screen content or collect personal data, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Needed in Auto mode only.

Good to know: some apps block screenshots for security, for example banking apps and some video apps. Long Snap cannot capture those screens.

FAQ

Questions about long screenshots and Long Snap

How do I take a long screenshot on Android?

Install Long Snap, tap "Auto scroll & stitch" and open the page you want to capture. Tap the floating button and Long Snap scrolls the page, captures each screen and joins them into one long image. It stops by itself at the end of the page.

If you would rather scroll yourself, use "Manual snap & stitch" and tap the button once for each screen.

Is there a long screenshot app for Android with no ads?

Yes. Long Snap has no banners, no pop-ups and no video ads. It also adds no watermark to your screenshots and does not ask you to create an account.

Is Long Snap free?

Yes. Long Snap is free to download on Google Play, and both capture modes, Auto and Manual, are free to use.

Does Long Snap put a watermark on my screenshots?

No. The saved image contains only what was on your screen.

How do I screenshot a whole WhatsApp chat?

Start Long Snap and tap "Auto scroll & stitch". Open the chat in WhatsApp and scroll to the point where you want the screenshot to begin. Tap the floating button and let Long Snap scroll through the conversation, then tap the button again when you have everything you need. The same steps work in Telegram, Messenger and most other chat apps.

What is the difference between Auto and Manual mode?

In Auto mode, Long Snap scrolls the page for you and captures as it goes. It is the fastest way to capture a long page.

In Manual mode, you scroll the page yourself and tap the button for each screen. Use it when you only want certain parts of a page, or in an app where automatic scrolling does not work well. Manual mode does not need the accessibility service.

What is the best way to take a scrolling screenshot if my phone has no built-in option?

Some phones include a scroll capture feature, but it depends on the phone maker, the Android version and the app you are in. Long Snap works on any phone running Android 8.0 or newer, and it gives you a manual mode for pages where automatic scrolling is not possible.

Why does Long Snap ask for the Accessibility permission?

Auto mode needs a way to scroll the page for you. Android only lets an app perform swipe gestures in other apps through an accessibility service, so Long Snap uses one for that single purpose.

The service does not read what is on your screen and does not collect personal data. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings, and Manual mode works without it.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. Capturing and stitching happen on your phone, and the finished image is saved only to your own gallery. Long Snap never uploads your screenshots or anything shown on your screen.

The app does send anonymous crash reports and usage statistics so bugs can be found and fixed. These contain no screen content. The privacy policy has the details.

Where are my long screenshots saved?

In your gallery, in the Pictures/LongSnap folder. You can also share the image to any app straight from the result screen.

Why can't Long Snap capture some apps?

Some apps block screenshots for security, for example banking apps and some video apps. Android shows a blank screen to any screenshot tool in those apps, so Long Snap cannot capture them.

Auto scroll does not start. What should I do?

Open Android Settings, go to Accessibility, find Long Snap and turn it off and on again. Android sometimes disconnects accessibility services after an app update. Manual mode keeps working in the meantime.

Which Android versions does Long Snap support?

Long Snap works on phones and tablets running Android 8.0 or newer.
